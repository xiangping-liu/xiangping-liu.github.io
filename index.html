Xiangping Liu

The University of Texas at Austin

Austin, Texas

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Biomedical Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Prof. Huiliang Evan Wang.

My research focuses on ultrasound-responsive nanomaterials, mechanoluminescence, sono-optogenetic neuromodulation, and therapeutic nanomaterials for neurological disorders.

I am particularly interested in developing minimally invasive materials and devices that convert externally applied ultrasound into optical, chemical, or biological signals.
